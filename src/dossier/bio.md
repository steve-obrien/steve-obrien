# Bio — Steven Andrew O'Brien

Bristol, UK • Founder, technologist, former rock musician
Born December 1983 *(sensitive — redact at export)*

## Summary

Steven Andrew O'Brien is a Bristol-based technology founder. He has self-disclosed that he was dyslexic at school, and credits computing — first encountered when he built a website for his teenage rock band — with giving him an externalised "memory and organisation system" that made academic learning workable. His self-described first software project was a 2003 "cloud system to connect people and data together" — a precursor to the Newicon model, built when he was around 20.

His engineering career was at Airbus Filton: a four-year Technical Apprenticeship (2002–06) including an HND in Aeronautical Engineering, followed by three years as a Modelling & Simulation Engineer (2006–09).

In parallel he incorporated **Newicon Ltd** in Bristol on **14 August 2006** as a side venture. The company publicly launched as a "Beta" in early 2009, when Steven went full-time — the date he typically gives as the founding year. He held both **CEO and CTO** titles concurrently for the company's full ~19-year run, growing it as a Bristol-based design-led software, AI-engineering and IoT studio across several offices in the BS16 and BS1 postcodes. Disclosed clients include **Airbus, Thales, University of the West of England, and Palladium**, plus SMEs.

In **April 2025** Newicon announced its merger / acquisition with **Linebreak (Technology UK) Ltd** (CH 14326547), the UK operating entity of a Patrick Nicolet–chaired Linebreak group (Linebreak AG, Linebreak Technology UK, Line Break Capital). Patrick Nicolet is a former Group CTO and Group Executive Board member at Capgemini SE; the group's UK CEO **Dolo Miah** was himself previously a Newicon co-officer / co-founder. Steven continued as CTO of New Icon under Linebreak through **31 January 2026**, when he formally resigned.

In addition to his technology career, Steven has a public musical record. He played Vocals / Rhythm Guitar in the Bristol four-piece **In Extremis** (~2004–2013, per Spotify catalog dates). The band's two known releases — *Killing Time* (EP, 2004) and *Somewhere but Here* (album, 2013) — appear to remain on Spotify.

Steven was also a director of **Alivio Botanicals Limited** (CH 12746813, Bristol food-services), a side venture incorporated July 2020 during the COVID era and now dissolved. (This directorship was found via Companies House officer search and did not surface in the initial interview.)

He was named in the **Insider South West 42 under 42 (2022 cohort)**. Newicon was also a **Founding Partner** of Bristol Technology Festival 2022, and has been a **TEDxBristol** sponsor since 2015 (and built the TEDxBristol website CMS).

A documented Newicon client engagement is **EarSwitch Ltd** (Bristol MedTech) — Newicon led the software development across both phases of an in-ear health-monitoring sensor that won £1.2M in investment and was selected for the MHRA/DHSC/NICE Innovative Device Access Programme (IDAP) — one of only 8 chosen technologies.

## Now (as of 2026-05-06)

Per his live personal site at [steve-obrien.com](https://steve-obrien.com), Steven describes himself as:

> "Engineer, founder, and builder of real-world AI systems. I spent 18 years building, scaling, then selling a software consultancy, leading complex projects across AI, cloud, and product engineering. Now I focus on designing and building practical AI-driven systems."

Current focus: *"Designing better developer workflows and shipping with clarity."* Operating remote-first, collaborating globally. Active prototypes include **Realtime Tools** (low-latency collaborative interfaces) and **AI-Augmented Docs** (documentation that adapts to user intent); **Design Tokens Lab** is paused. He continues to list "New Icon" among his current projects, suggesting an ongoing advisory / strategy relationship post-departure.
