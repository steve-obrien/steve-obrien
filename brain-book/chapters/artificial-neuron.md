## The analogy

An artificial “neuron” receives numbers, weights them, combines them, and passes a transformed value onward. The name is historically understandable and intuitively helpful. It is also dangerous when the metaphor is mistaken for equivalence.

## Questions to develop

- Which properties of biological neurons inspired the abstraction?
- Which important properties disappeared?
- When does simplification make engineering possible?
- Does intelligence depend on reproducing biology, or only on reproducing some of its principles?
